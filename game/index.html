EMBER DUEL

A turn-based battle card game. Outwit the Ash Baron, card by card.

How to play

  • Each side has 30 life. First to 0 loses.
  • Each turn you gain mana (1 more each turn, up to 10) and draw a card.
  • Click a card in your hand (or press 1–7) to play it. Creatures fight; spells strike, heal or draw.
  • Click your creature, then an enemy to attack. Guards (🛡) must be attacked first.
  • Press Space or click End Turn when done.

An original game — Ember Duel. No network, no accounts, just cards.
